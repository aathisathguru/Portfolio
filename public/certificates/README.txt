Place your certificate images in this folder:

public/certificates/
├── certificate-01.png
├── certificate-02.png
├── certificate-03.png
└── certificate-04.png

Supported formats: PNG, JPG, WEBP.
Recommended aspect ratio: Landscape (~4:3 or 16:10 or 1.414:1 A4 landscape).
When you add your files here, the portfolio gallery will automatically load and display them!
